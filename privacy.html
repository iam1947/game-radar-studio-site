Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Game Radar Studio is a local desktop workflow tool used to create short videos about free game giveaways and upload them to connected creator accounts as drafts.

Data we process

The app may process local video files, generated metadata, publication titles, descriptions, hashtags, and OAuth tokens required to connect the user's own social accounts.

How data is used

Data is used only to render videos, prepare publication metadata, and upload videos to the authorized user's own account.

Data sharing

Game Radar Studio does not sell personal data. Data is sent to third-party platforms only when the user explicitly authorizes an account and starts an upload.
